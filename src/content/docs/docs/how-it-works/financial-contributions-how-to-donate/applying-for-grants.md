Use this guide when a funder may support your Collective through OSE. Your Collective leads the application and project; OSE reviews its own role and commitments.

## The grant journey

1. **Before submitting:** check the funder's requirements and contact OSE. If the application names OSE as recipient or signatory, OSE must review and accept that role before submission.
2. **If awarded:** share the grant agreement and arrange the necessary agreements and payment instructions with OSE.
3. **When funds arrive:** use the Collective budget in line with the funding conditions. A promised grant is not an available balance.
4. **During the project:** prepare activity reports and project monitoring. OSE provides accounting information it holds and validates what engages OSE.

The detailed agreement, communication and reporting requirements follow below. See also [hosting fees](/docs/faq/general-faqs/#how-much-does-it-cost) and [spending the grant budget](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/).

## Overview

Open Source Europe (OSE) enables hosted Collectives to receive grants from institutional and philanthropic funders, both in Europe and internationally. Our team helps you prepare the arrangements for receiving and managing the grant.

:::danger
**Important**: If a grant application names OSE as recipient or signatory, contact OSE **before submission** so the team can review and accept its role and commitments.
:::

***

## Initial Steps

1. **Contact OSE early**

   Email <oce@opencollective.com> to let us know about the grant opportunity. We'll help assess whether it's compatible with fiscal hosting through OSE.
2. **Check funder requirements**

   Not all funders allow grants to be awarded to fiscally sponsored initiatives. Be sure to confirm with them whether a hosted structure is accepted.
3. **For U.S. funders**

   Contact the OSE team to arrange the documentation required by your funder. See [Equivalency Determination Certificate](/docs/what-we-offer/equivalency-determination-certificate/).

***

## Grant Agreements & Receiving Funds

1. **Grant Agreement**

   The funder will provide a grant agreement. This must be signed by the confirmed recipient entity.\
   Please share the agreement with us via <oce@opencollective.com>, or have the funder reach out to us directly.
2. **Mandate Agreement**

   Your initiative will also need to sign a Mandate Agreement with OSE.\
   This formalizes the arrangement and must be signed by two members of your initiative.
3. **Bank Transfer**

   The OSE team will provide the appropriate bank transfer instructions for the agreed grant arrangement.
4. **Allocation on the Platform**

   Once the grant is received, the funds will appear on your Collective’s Open Collective page as available balance.

***

## Funder Communication

* **Acknowledgments**

  You can publicly recognize funders on your Collective page. Their name will appear in your list of contributors unless they choose to remain anonymous.
* **Updates**

  Keep your funders informed by posting regular updates through the Open Collective platform.
* **Ongoing Communication**

  Any requests or messages from funders sent to OSE will be forwarded to your initiative for follow-up.

***

## Grant Reporting

The Collective prepares activity reports and project monitoring. OSE provides the accounting information it holds and validates statements or commitments that engage OSE. Follow the reporting requirements and deadlines of the grant agreement.

***

## Need Help?

The Collective leads its own fundraising. OSE may occasionally share opportunities or make introductions, according to its capacity; it does not promise a structured fundraising service or funding success. For questions about receiving and administering a grant, contact <oce@opencollective.com>.
