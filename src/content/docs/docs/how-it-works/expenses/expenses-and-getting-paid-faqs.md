For the steps from planning a purchase to payment, start with the [expense guide](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/). This page answers specific payment questions.

### How does paying people work?

Submit an expense through the Collective’s page. Expenses are reviewed by the Collective and OSE. If the sole administrator requests their own reimbursement, OSE can check the supporting documents and pay without a second approver on the Collective side.

We aim to initiate payment within seven business days once the expense is complete and approved and the necessary funds have been received and are available. Bank processing time is additional. This is a target, not a guaranteed date of receipt.

***

### How do I get paid / reimbursed / receive my grant money?

Go to your initiative's page and click **“Submit Expense”**. You’ll need to log in (or create an account), then upload a valid receipt or invoice.

:::note
[How to submit an expense – Open Collective Docs](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/expenses-and-getting-paid/submitting-expenses)
:::

***

### Invoice or Reimbursement – which one do I need?

* **Reimbursement**: Use this when you've paid out-of-pocket. A receipt is required (must include vendor name, address, and tax ID). Order confirmations are not valid receipts.
* **Invoice**: Use this when you're requesting payment for services. A formal invoice is required, including contact details, tax info, and description of services.

:::note
[Invoices vs. Reimbursements – OSE Docs](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/invoices-or-reimbursements/)
:::

***

### Can we pay people for their time?

Collectives can pay service providers against a compliant invoice, including through a workers’ cooperative. Agree the work, price and dates with the Collective and submit the invoice through Open Collective for review. OSE does not provide a standard employment or payroll service. Any arrangement requiring OSE to sign a specific contract must be discussed and accepted beforehand.

### Which documents are needed?

Invoices must be addressed to the Collective or the person who placed the order. OSE acts only as the payer for these purchases. Collectives must not use OSE’s legal name or VAT number to have invoices issued.

Reimbursements require an invoice or receipt. A bank statement alone is insufficient. See the [expense guide](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/) and [Invoices or Reimbursements?](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/invoices-or-reimbursements/).

***

### Can I donate part of my Collective’s budget to a charity?

Ordinary external donations may be made to identified nonprofit organisations after OSE has reviewed and approved the beneficiary, purpose and funding conditions. Contact OSE before committing or requesting payment. Other recipient types require a specific assessment, without any promise of acceptance. Once OSE has confirmed the arrangement, the beneficiary submits the request with the following documents:

* A donation request letter signed by one of their administrators, stating the amount and purpose of the donation
* Their incorporation document
* Their bank identification details

:::danger
A donation is distinct from payment for goods or services. It may still carry restrictions on use and reporting obligations. If a benefit or service is promised in return, discuss the arrangement with OSE before proceeding.
:::

***

### Are receipts and invoices public?

Only general information (amount, description, purpose) is public. Uploaded documents (invoices, receipts) are private — visible only to the Collective’s admins and OSE staff.
