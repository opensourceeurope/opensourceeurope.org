## Choose your payment

| Your situation                                             | Where to start                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| You have already paid for something for the Collective     | [Prepare a reimbursement](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/invoices-or-reimbursements/#prepare-a-reimbursement)      |
| A supplier needs to be paid for goods or services          | [Prepare an invoice](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/invoices-or-reimbursements/#prepare-an-invoice)                |
| The payment is a donation or grant to another organisation | [Ask OSE about an outgoing grant or donation](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/request-a-grant-for-nonprofits-only/) |

## Before you submit

* Have the invoice or receipt and the details needed for payment ready. A bank statement alone is insufficient for reimbursement.
* Check that the necessary funds have been received and are available.
* For supplier invoices, use the Collective's name or the person who placed the order; do not use OSE's legal name or VAT number.
* If eligibility is unclear, ask OSE before spending. A specific contract signed by OSE requires advance agreement.

**Ready?** Go to [Submission Process](#submission-process). **Already submitted?** Read [Approval Workflow](#approval-workflow), or use the expense comments for routine follow-up. Send sensitive information privately.

**Payment target:** seven business days once the expense is complete and approved and funds are available; bank processing time is additional. This is not a guaranteed receipt date.

## Overview

Managing expenses with Open Source Europe (OSE) is designed to be transparent and straightforward. Whether you're reimbursing a team member or paying a service provider, the process ensures accountability and compliance with fiscal regulations.

***

## Before spending and requesting reimbursement

OSE does not require systematic advance approval for ordinary expenses. If eligibility is unclear, contact the team before spending. Reimbursement remains subject to review. Any arrangement requiring a specific contract signed by OSE must be discussed and accepted in advance.

If someone cannot advance the cost, direct payment to the supplier on a compliant invoice is possible. OSE does not provide advances to individuals.

***

## Types of Expenses

There are two primary types of expenses you can submit:

### 1. **Reimbursements**

Used when someone has paid out-of-pocket for goods or services on behalf of the Collective.

* **Required Documentation**: **Fiscal receipts**
  * Vendor's name, address and tax reference
  * Transaction date
  * Description of items or services
  * Amount paid

:::caution
**Note**: Order confirmations, proforma invoices, or bank statements are not valid receipts. Learn more: [How to Submit a Reimbursement](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/expenses-and-getting-paid/reimbursement)
:::

### 2. **Invoices**

Used when paying individuals or organizations for services rendered.

* **Required Documentation**:
  * Unique invoice number
  * Service provider's full details (name, address, tax ID)
  * Detailed description of services
  * Applicable tax rate and amount
  * Invoice addressed to the Collective or the person who placed the order

:::danger
**Important**: Invoices must be addressed to the Collective or the person who placed the order. OSE acts only as the payer for these purchases. Collectives must not use OSE’s legal name or VAT number to have invoices issued. Learn more: [How to Submit an Invoice](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/expenses-and-getting-paid/invoices)
:::

***

## Supporting documents and timing of submission

Without an invoice or receipt, OSE refuses reimbursement; a bank statement alone is insufficient. For durable equipment, the usual supporting documents are required; OSE does not systematically require an additional inventory. Specific funding or agreement requirements still apply.

There is no general fixed deadline for submitting a reimbursement, but deadlines attached to a funding agreement or closure must be respected. Expenses incurred before the Collective was accepted by OSE may be reimbursed after review of their connection to the project, documents and funding conditions, provided funds are available.

***

## Paying service providers

Collectives can pay service providers against a compliant invoice, including through a workers’ cooperative. Agree the work, price and dates with the Collective and submit the invoice through Open Collective for review. OSE does not provide a standard employment or payroll service. Any arrangement requiring OSE to sign a specific contract must be discussed and accepted beforehand.

***

## Submission Process

1. Go to your Collective's page and click **“Submit Expense”**
2. Choose the expense type: **Reimbursement** or **Invoice**
3. Fill in the required details and upload documentation
4. Select a payout method (e.g., PayPal, Wise/bank transfer)
5. Submit for approval

:::note
**Processing Time**: We aim to initiate payment within seven business days once the expense is complete and approved and the necessary funds have been received and are available. Bank processing time is additional. This is a target, not a guaranteed date of receipt.
:::

***

## Approval Workflow

Expenses are reviewed by the Collective and OSE. If the sole administrator requests their own reimbursement, OSE can check the supporting documents and pay without a second approver on the Collective side.

OSE checks the documents before payment. Incomplete or non-compliant expenses may be rejected. A promised donation or grant is not an available balance: funds must be received and available before payment.

***

## Payment Methods

Supported payout options:

* **PayPal** (must be active and verified)
* **Bank Transfer via Wise** (international bank details required)

:::danger
**Caution**: Ensure that payout details match the expense documentation to avoid delays.
:::

***

## Compliance and Best Practices

* **Transparency**: All expenses are publicly visible to ensure accountability.
* **Accuracy**: Double-check amounts, details, and receipts before submitting.
* **Communication**: Use expense comments for routine questions and follow-up. Contact OSE privately for sensitive information; do not put sensitive documents or bank details in comments.

:::note
**Need help?** Email us at <oce@opencollective.com>
:::
