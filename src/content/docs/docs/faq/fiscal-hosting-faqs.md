### What is a Fiscal Host?

A **Fiscal Host** is an organization that holds funds on behalf of Collectives, allowing them to operate financially without needing to establish a legal entity.

This arrangement — also called fiscal sponsorship — allows your Collective to:

* Receive and manage donations and grants
* Provide donation confirmations and administer grants
* Handle taxes, bookkeeping, and legal compliance
* Pay expenses approved by your Collective admins

The Open Collective platform provides all the tools to manage these flows transparently.

***

### How do I add a Fiscal Host to my Collective?

If your Collective does not yet have a Fiscal Host, you can add one by:

1. Going to your Collective page
2. Clicking on **“Apply to Fiscal Host”**
3. Choosing the appropriate Host (such as Open Source Europe)

:::note
Learn how: [Add a Fiscal Host](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/collectives/add-fiscal-host)
:::

### Moving to another fiscal host

Contact OSE before leaving to coordinate with the new host, settle outstanding expenses and check the conditions for transferring the balance. Transfers remain subject to the hosting agreement and restrictions on the funds. A change on Open Collective does not automatically change your GitHub Sponsors setup.

### Closing a Collective voluntarily

If the Collective stops operating without moving to another host, settle outstanding expenses first. The Collective proposes a destination for the remaining balance consistent with its purpose; OSE reviews and approves it according to the funding conditions.

### Inactive Collectives

For prolonged inactivity, OSE contacts the administrators and sends reminders, stating a response deadline and the consequences of no reply. Without a response, OSE may end hosting, stop recurring contributions and reallocate the balance to its general fund or a similar project, under the applicable agreement and funding restrictions. There is no universal six-month timeline. This process is separate from unidentified incoming payments.
