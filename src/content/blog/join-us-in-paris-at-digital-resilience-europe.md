Open Source Europe hosts many open source projects, but most of them have not met each other yet. We want to change that. In the coming months, we want to connect our collectives, share what we have learned, and plan our roadmap for 2027 together.

We will start by meeting in person.

## Digital Resilience Europe, part of FOST Paris

We will be at [Digital Resilience Europe](https://digitalresilienceeurope.org/) on 2-3 December 2026, at CNIT Forest in Paris. The event is part of FOST Paris. Its topics are the resilience of open ecosystems, open source sustainability, and API ecosystems. Two other events, [Europe API](https://europeapi.com/) and [API Standards](https://api-standards.joinfost.io/), take place at the same time.

## Call for speakers

Do you have experience with digital resilience, open source maintenance, security, or keeping an ecosystem sustainable? Please send a talk proposal. We would love to see people from our collectives on stage.

The deadline is close, so please [send your proposal](https://apidays.typeform.com/to/xNJoBOMt) soon.

## A booth for your projects on both days

Open Source Europe will have a booth on both days of the event. We want the booth to be your space too. Bring stickers, flyers, or anything else that shows your project. At the booth you can meet other maintainers and talk about funding, governance, and common problems. We also want your feedback on the future of Open Source Europe, so we can plan 2027 together.

The booth is not only for promoting Open Source Europe. It is also for promoting the collectives we host.

## 50 free tickets

We have 50 free tickets for community members and maintainers. To get a ticket, send an email to [home@opensourceeurope.org](mailto:home@opensourceeurope.org). Tell us about your project and your role in it. We give out the tickets in the order we receive the emails.

We hope to see you in Paris!
