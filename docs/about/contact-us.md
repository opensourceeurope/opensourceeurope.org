## Choose the right contact

The following table lists where to go for each kind of question:

| You need help with                                             | What to do                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A submitted expense                                            | Use its comments on Open Collective for routine questions. Send sensitive information privately to OSE. |
| Hosting, a funding arrangement, or another OSE question        | Email <home@opensourceeurope.org>.                                                                         |
| Suspected misuse of funds or an issue involving administrators | Contact OSE directly in private. You do not need to go through those administrators.                    |
| A safeguarding concern                                         | Use the [safeguarding contacts and procedure](/docs/about/safeguarding-policy/).        |
| A technical problem with Open Collective                       | Use [platform support](https://opencollective.com/help).                                                |

OSE aims to answer general questions within seven business days. The seven days are a target, not a guaranteed response time. It does not replace the specific guidance for applications, payments, or safeguarding.

## Expense follow-up and sensitive concerns

Use comments on the expense for routine questions about a submitted expense. The comments are not public. The expense submitter, the Collective's administrators, and the fiscal host's administrators can see them. Email <home@opensourceeurope.org> directly for sensitive information that the Collective's administrators should not see.

For suspected misuse of funds or a problem involving Collective administrators, contact the OSE team directly in private at <home@opensourceeurope.org>. You do not need to go through the administrators concerned.

## Internal conflicts

Collectives manage their own internal conflicts. OSE intervenes when the situation affects funds or hosting conditions. It does not promise mediation.
