Open Source Europe (OSE) is a Belgian association. It is the fiscal host for open source initiatives and works with philanthropic institutions to support regranting around the world.

***

## Service overview

Unlike traditional nonprofits, OSE combines a legal structure with its own digital infrastructure. Together they make the following possible:

* Fiscal hosting for open source projects. OSE gives developers, maintainers, and communities a legal umbrella, so they can operate without creating their own legal entity.
* Grant distribution and fund management. OSE manages funds for foundations and companies that want to support critical open source projects in Europe and beyond.
* Radical transparency. All financial activity is public on the Open Collective platform, which builds trust and accountability in the community.

***

## How hosting works

OSE provides a Belgian nonprofit home for open source projects. OSE uses the Open Collective platform to manage budgets, receive contributions, and process expenses transparently.

For the hosting agreement, see the [Terms of Service](/docs/terms-of-service/).

***

## Who OSE serves

OSE works with the following groups:

* Project maintainers of open source projects that have no legal structure of their own.
* Funders and corporates, such as international companies that want a trusted intermediary to support open source in Europe.
* Developer networks, meaning communities that want fiscal and operational support for their collective activities.

***

## How to work with OSE

You can work with OSE in the following ways:

* Projects can apply for fiscal hosting to start receiving donations and grants legally.
* Funders can support projects and manage their contributions transparently through the funds that OSE hosts.
* Organizations can manage large-scale distributions efficiently, without complex international structures.

## A little history

Open Source Europe grew out of Open Collective Europe's fiscal hosting work. As that work grew, OSE became the home for open source projects and communities. Open Collective Europe Foundation developed its wider philanthropic activities. The OSE hosting agreement still uses the association's name, Open Collective Europe ASBL/vzw.

Today, OSE focuses on helping open source communities receive funding, pay contributors, and manage their projects transparently.
