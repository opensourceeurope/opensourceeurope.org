## Understand OSE

Read [what OSE offers](/docs/what-we-offer/), explore [budget decisions](/docs/guides/deciding-on-how-to-use-your-money/), or consult the [payment FAQ](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/expenses-and-getting-paid-faqs/).

For a safeguarding concern, go directly to the [safeguarding policy and contacts](/docs/about/safeguarding-policy/).

Open Source Europe is a Belgian Association (ASBL/vzw). Its tech platform lets over 400 open source groups raise and spend more than 1 million euros a year, all in full transparency.

### Our Mission

We support movements and decentralized communities by providing the infrastructure needed to:

* Launch new projects.
* Manage and oversee financial operations.
* Keep open source initiatives legally and fiscally compliant.

If you are an open source project, OSE helps you reach your goals by taking on the administrative work of managing your funds.

We also work with foundations and philanthropists to set up dedicated funds. These funds let them re-grant resources to open source initiatives globally.

***

### How We Can Help

#### For Open Source Collectives

By partnering with OSE, your group can:

* Launch and manage projects without having to incorporate a separate legal entity.
* Simplify financial operations with the Open Collective platform.
* Connect with global philanthropic opportunities and corporate sponsors.

#### For Foundations and Philanthropists

We offer professional help with:

* Creating and managing philanthropic funds.
* Supporting transparent re-granting processes.
* Supporting open source initiatives on a global scale through a recognized European structure.
