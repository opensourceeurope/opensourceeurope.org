## Understand OSE

Read [what OSE offers](/docs/what-we-offer/), explore [budget decisions](/docs/guides/deciding-on-how-to-use-your-money/), or consult the [payment FAQ](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/expenses-and-getting-paid-faqs/).

For a safeguarding concern, go directly to the [safeguarding policy and contacts](/docs/about/safeguarding-policy/).

Open Source Europe is a Belgian association (ASBL/vzw). Its tech platform lets open source groups raise and spend funds in full transparency.

### Mission

OSE supports movements and decentralized communities with the infrastructure they need to:

* Launch new projects
* Manage and oversee financial operations
* Keep open source initiatives legally and fiscally compliant

If you run an open source project, OSE helps you reach your goals. OSE takes on the administrative work of managing your funds.

OSE also works with foundations and philanthropists to set up dedicated funds. These funds let them re-grant resources to open source initiatives globally.

***

### How OSE can help

#### For open source Collectives

When your group partners with OSE, it can:

* Launch and manage projects without having to incorporate a separate legal entity
* Simplify financial operations with the Open Collective platform
* Connect with global philanthropic opportunities and corporate sponsors

#### For foundations and philanthropists

OSE offers professional help to:

* Support transparent re-granting processes
* Create and manage philanthropic funds
* Support open source initiatives on a global scale through a recognized European structure
