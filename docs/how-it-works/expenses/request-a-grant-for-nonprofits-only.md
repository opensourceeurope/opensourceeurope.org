Besides expenses and invoices, the Open Collective platform also has a **Request a Grant** option.

Collectives may also request approval for donations to identified nonprofit organizations. This permission is separate from access to the **Request a Grant** feature that this page describes. Open Source Europe (OSE) reviews the beneficiary, purpose, and funding conditions before it accepts an outgoing grant or donation.

The **Request a Grant** feature is not available to all Collectives, and its use must follow specific conditions.

***

## Who can use “Request a Grant”?

* This guide covers grant requests to OSE Funds. Whether a profile can receive grant requests depends on the expense types enabled for it. These expense types alone do not make a request eligible. OSE confirms the right way to submit for the arrangement you agreed.
* The ordinary OSE route is for identified nonprofit organizations, such as foundations, associations sans but lucratif (ASBLs), charities, or nongovernmental organizations (NGOs). OSE must first review and agree to the beneficiary, purpose, and funding conditions.
* Other beneficiaries, including individuals or informal collectives, need a specific assessment by OSE, and OSE does not guarantee to accept them. Contact OSE before you go ahead. A request is not eligible only because the platform offers the feature.

***

## Requirements for submitting a grant request

When a nonprofit uses the **Request a Grant** feature, it must include the following documents:

* Copy of the nonprofit's legal bylaws (*statuts*)
* Signed letter that officially requests the grant and sets out:
  * The amount, purpose, and objectives of the requested funding
  * How the nonprofit plans to use the funds in line with the Fund’s mission
  * The legal name and contact details of the nonprofit organization
* Bank account holder statement of the nonprofit

> These documents help OSE check legal eligibility, check that the request meets the funder requirements, and prepare reporting if needed.

***

## Internal review process

Who selects the projects or beneficiaries depends on the program. The selection roles are agreed with OSE when the Fund is set up. Selection does not replace OSE's review and agreement before payment.

The OSE team reviews all grant requests manually. For an approved nonprofit grant, OSE transfers the funds directly to the nonprofit's official bank account. OSE records the transaction transparently on the Fund page. For other beneficiaries, OSE must first confirm whether it can accept the arrangement, and which documents and payment arrangements it needs.

For questions about an OSE Fund or about who can receive a grant, contact <home@opensourceeurope.org>.
