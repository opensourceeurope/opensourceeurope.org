Choose the request type before you submit an expense. Use **Reimbursement** if you have already paid. Use **Invoice** when a supplier asks for payment for goods or services.

## Submit your request

1. Choose the matching document checklist on this page: [Prepare an invoice](#prepare-an-invoice) or [Prepare a reimbursement](#prepare-a-reimbursement).
2. Open your Collective on Open Collective and choose **Submit Expense**.
3. Select the type, complete the details, attach the supporting document, and choose the payout method.
4. Submit the expense for review by the Collective and Open Source Europe (OSE). Follow the [approval and payment guidance](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/#approval-workflow).

If someone cannot pay the cost first, OSE can pay the supplier directly on a compliant invoice. OSE does not provide advances to individuals. OSE pays only after a review, and only when the funds are available.

For a donation or grant to another organization, use the [outgoing grants guide](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/request-a-grant-for-nonprofits-only/). Do not treat a donation or grant as a supplier invoice.

## The difference between the two types

When you submit an expense to your Collective, choose the correct type.

## Prepare an invoice

Use an invoice to request payment for services or goods you have provided.

An invoice has the following characteristics:

* Freelancers, contractors, and companies submit invoices.
* Invoices pay for goods or services, including a supplier deposit. For a deposit, OSE must first review and agree to the documents and conditions in advance, and the funds must be available.
* A formal invoice needs the following details:
  * Unique invoice number
  * Full identification (name, address, and tax or company number)
  * Detailed description of the service or goods
  * Applicable tax rate and amount. If the tax rate is zero, put a legal reference for the exemption on the invoice itself (for example, value-added tax (VAT) exemption according to article YY of law XX).
  * Addressed to the Collective or the person who placed the order, with the appropriate recipient details

:::danger
Address invoices to the Collective or the person who placed the order. OSE acts only as the payer for these purchases. Collectives must not ask for invoices in OSE’s legal name or with OSE's VAT number.
:::

***

## Prepare a reimbursement

Use a reimbursement when you have paid for something for the Collective with your own money and want the money back.

A reimbursement has the following characteristics:

* Members or contributors of the Collective submit reimbursements.
* Reimbursements pay back purchases made for the Collective.
* A reimbursement needs a valid receipt from the provider that shows:
  * Vendor's name and address
  * Transaction date
  * Description of items or services
  * Amount paid

:::caution
Order confirmations, proforma invoices, and bank or PayPal statements are not valid receipts.
:::

***

## Special cases

If you ask for reimbursement of only part of a receipt, highlight the relevant items on the document itself.

:::tip
Sometimes the breakdown is not obvious, such as a percentage or a split. In that case, explain the calculation clearly in your request and attach the supporting receipt. OSE may ask for clarification. As a rule, OSE does not require a signed and dated note.
:::

OSE refuses reimbursement without an invoice or receipt. A bank statement alone is not enough. For the rules on prior spending, submission timing, available funds, and payment targets, see [Expenses](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/).
