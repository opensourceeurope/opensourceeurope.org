For the steps from planning a purchase to payment, start with the [expense guide](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/). This page answers specific payment questions.

## How does paying people work?

Submit an expense through the Collective’s page. The Collective and Open Source Europe (OSE) review expenses. If the sole administrator requests their own reimbursement, OSE can check the supporting documents and pay without a second approver on the Collective side.

OSE aims to start the payment within seven business days. These days count from when the expense is complete and approved, and the Collective has received the necessary funds and has them available. Bank processing time comes on top. The seven days are a target, not a guaranteed date of receipt.

***

## How do I get paid / reimbursed / receive my grant money?

Go to your Collective's page and click **Submit Expense**. Log in or create an account. Then upload a valid receipt or invoice.

:::note
For more information, see [How to submit an expense](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/expenses-and-getting-paid/submitting-expenses) in the Open Collective docs.
:::

***

## Invoice or reimbursement: which one do I need?

Choose the expense type that matches your situation:

* If you paid with your own money, use a reimbursement. You need a receipt that shows the vendor's name, address, and tax ID. Order confirmations are not valid receipts.
* When you request payment for services, use an invoice. You need a formal invoice with contact details, tax information, and a description of the services.

:::note
For more information, see [Invoices or reimbursements?](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/invoices-or-reimbursements/).
:::

***

## Can we pay people for their time?

Collectives can pay service providers against a compliant invoice. This includes payment through a workers’ cooperative. Agree the work, price, and dates with the Collective. Then submit the invoice through Open Collective for review. OSE does not provide a standard employment or payroll service. If an arrangement requires OSE to sign a specific contract, OSE must discuss and accept it beforehand.

## Which documents are needed?

Address invoices to the Collective or the person who placed the order. OSE acts only as the payer for these purchases. Collectives must not ask for invoices in OSE’s legal name or with OSE's value-added tax (VAT) number.

Reimbursements require an invoice or receipt. A bank statement alone is not enough. For more information, see the [expense guide](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/) and [Invoices or reimbursements?](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/invoices-or-reimbursements/).

***

## Can I donate part of my Collective’s budget to a charity?

A Collective can make ordinary external donations to identified nonprofit organizations. OSE must first review and approve the beneficiary, purpose, and funding conditions. Contact OSE before you make a commitment or request payment. Other types of recipient require a specific assessment, and OSE does not promise to accept them. Once OSE has confirmed the arrangement, the beneficiary submits the request with the following documents:

* A donation request letter, signed by one of the beneficiary's administrators, that states the amount and purpose of the donation
* Incorporation document of the beneficiary
* Bank identification details of the beneficiary

:::danger
A donation is not the same as a payment for goods or services. A donation can still come with limits on how the money is used, and with reporting obligations. If the donation comes with a promised benefit or service in return, discuss the arrangement with OSE before you go ahead.
:::

***

## Are receipts and invoices public?

The public budget shows an expense’s title, amount, and public display name. Do not put sensitive information in the title. The public cannot see attached receipts, detailed descriptions, or payment details. Who can see these details depends on the information and the platform permissions. Access is not limited to OSE staff. For more information, see [Open Collective’s reimbursement privacy guidance](https://documentation.opencollective.com/expenses-and-getting-paid/submitting-expenses/submitting-a-reimbursement) and [expense-submission guidance](https://documentation.opencollective.com/expenses-and-getting-paid/submitting-expenses).

## Need help?

Use the comments on the expense for routine questions and follow-up. Expense comments are not public. The expense submitter, the Collective’s administrators, and the fiscal host’s administrators can see them. For sensitive information that the Collective’s administrators should not see, email OSE directly at <home@opensourceeurope.org>. For more information, see [Open Collective’s expense-comment guidance](https://documentation.opencollective.com/expenses-and-getting-paid/editing-an-expense).
