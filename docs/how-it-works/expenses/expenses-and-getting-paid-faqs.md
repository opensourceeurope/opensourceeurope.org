For the steps from planning a purchase to payment, start with the [expense guide](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/). This page answers specific payment questions.

## How does paying people work?

Submit an expense through the Collective’s page. Expenses are reviewed by the Collective and OSE. If the sole administrator requests their own reimbursement, OSE can check the supporting documents and pay without a second approver on the Collective side.

We aim to start the payment within seven business days. These days count from when the expense is complete and approved, and the necessary funds have been received and are available. Bank processing time comes on top. This is a target, not a guaranteed date of receipt.

***

## How do I get paid / reimbursed / receive my grant money?

Go to your initiative's page and click **“Submit Expense”**. You’ll need to log in (or create an account), then upload a valid receipt or invoice.

:::note
[How to submit an expense – Open Collective Docs](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/expenses-and-getting-paid/submitting-expenses)
:::

***

## Invoice or Reimbursement – which one do I need?

* **Reimbursement**: Use this when you paid with your own money. You need a receipt that shows the vendor's name, address and tax ID. Order confirmations are not valid receipts.
* **Invoice**: Use this when you're requesting payment for services. You need a formal invoice with contact details, tax information and a description of the services.

:::note
[Invoices vs. Reimbursements – OSE Docs](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/invoices-or-reimbursements/)
:::

## Can we pay people for their time?

Collectives can pay service providers against a compliant invoice. This includes payment through a workers’ cooperative. Agree the work, price and dates with the Collective and submit the invoice through Open Collective for review. OSE does not provide a standard employment or payroll service. If an arrangement requires OSE to sign a specific contract, it must be discussed and accepted beforehand.

## Which documents are needed?

Invoices must be addressed to the Collective or the person who placed the order. OSE acts only as the payer for these purchases. Collectives must not use OSE’s legal name or VAT number to have invoices issued.

Reimbursements require an invoice or receipt. A bank statement alone is not enough. See the [expense guide](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/) and [Invoices or Reimbursements?](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/invoices-or-reimbursements/).

***

## Can I donate part of my Collective’s budget to a charity?

Ordinary external donations may be made to identified nonprofit organisations after OSE has reviewed and approved the beneficiary, purpose and funding conditions. Contact OSE before committing or requesting payment. Other types of recipient require a specific assessment, and OSE does not promise to accept them. Once OSE has confirmed the arrangement, the beneficiary submits the request with the following documents:

* A donation request letter signed by one of their administrators, stating the amount and purpose of the donation
* Their incorporation document
* Their bank identification details

:::danger
A donation is not the same as a payment for goods or services. It may still come with limits on how the money is used, and with reporting obligations. If a benefit or service is promised in return, discuss the arrangement with OSE before you go ahead.
:::

***

## Are receipts and invoices public?

Only general information (amount, description, purpose) is public. Uploaded documents (invoices, receipts) are private. Only the Collective’s admins and OSE staff can see them.
