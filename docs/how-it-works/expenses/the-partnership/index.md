## Legal relationship: indirect representation

The relationship between your Collective and Open Source Europe (OSE) is one of indirect representation (*représentation indirecte* / *middellijke vertegenwoordiging*).

This relationship means the following:

* Your Collective remains fully responsible for its own activities, including legal and tax compliance.
* OSE acts as a fiscal host and intermediary, not as the legal representative or owner of your funds or operations.

:::danger
Your Collective must not use OSE's tax identification number, such as its value-added tax (VAT) number, to invoice or to register. If you need a tax number, you are responsible for getting your own tax number under local law.
:::

***

## Invoicing guidelines

Follow these rules to stay compliant with the OSE hosting terms:

* Address invoices to the Collective or the person who placed the order. OSE acts only as the payer for these purchases. Collectives must not ask for invoices in OSE’s legal name or with OSE's VAT number.
* Third parties must treat your Collective as an unincorporated group or individual for VAT purposes.
* If VAT does not apply, the invoice must cite the relevant VAT exemption article.

:::danger
If an invoice is wrongly addressed to OSE, the invoice must be canceled and replaced. Repeated errors may lead to the end of your fiscal hosting agreement.
:::

## Donations from your Collective

A Collective can make ordinary external donations to identified nonprofit organizations. OSE must first review and approve the beneficiary, purpose, and funding conditions. Contact OSE before you make a commitment or request payment. Other types of recipient require a specific assessment, and OSE does not promise to accept them.

Some funds come under a grant agreement or for a restricted purpose. Before OSE passes on such funds, OSE checks whether the transfer is permitted and which obligations still apply. Any required funder approval must be obtained. A transfer does not automatically release OSE from its responsibilities under the original funding agreement.

After OSE has approved the arrangement, follow these steps:

1. Ask the beneficiary to submit an expense through your Collective's page.
2. Make sure the expense includes the following documents:
   * Signed letter from the beneficiary that confirms the purpose and amount
   * Copy of the beneficiary's incorporation document
   * Bank identification document of the beneficiary

:::danger
The ordinary external donation route is for identified nonprofit organizations. Other cases need a specific assessment, and OSE does not accept them automatically. OSE does not treat a payment in exchange for goods, services, or visibility as an ordinary donation. Contact OSE before you make that commitment.
:::

***

## VAT and nonprofit status

For purchases by a Collective, the supplier must issue invoices to the Collective or the person who placed the order. OSE acts only as the payer. Do not use OSE’s legal name or VAT number.

VAT treatment depends on the specific transaction and the rules that apply to it. Do not assume one rule applies to every purchase. Ask the supplier or an accountant about exemptions, reverse charge, or VAT recovery.

:::note
For more information, see [VAT in Europe and how it works in OSE](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/the-partnership/vat-in-europe-and-how-it-works-in-ose/).
:::
