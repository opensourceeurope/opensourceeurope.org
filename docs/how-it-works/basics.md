This is your starting point after OSE accepts your Collective. Work through the essentials, then use the relevant guide for each transaction.

## Your first steps

1. **Check your public profile and contacts.** Use the platform's [Quick Start Guide](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/collectives/quick-start-guide). Keep administrator access and the main contact up to date, and notify OSE of changes.
2. **Choose how to receive funds.** Start with [donations and funding routes](/docs/how-it-works/financial-contributions-how-to-donate/). You can receive contributions without waiting for the optional welcome call.
3. **Agree how your Collective makes spending decisions.** Read [budget decisions](/docs/guides/deciding-on-how-to-use-your-money/).
4. **Prepare your first payment.** Use [Pay and get reimbursed](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/); funds must be received and available before payment.
5. **Keep the community informed.** Use Updates and review the responsibilities below.

**Need OSE's agreement first?** Contact the team before a grant application naming OSE as recipient or signatory, a specific contract OSE would sign, or a proposed commercial arrangement. Details follow below.

## Welcome to Open Source Europe!

Once your application to be hosted by Open Source Europe (OSE) is approved, you can immediately start managing your Collective on the Open Collective platform. Welcome aboard!

***

## Top 5 Things to Remember

1. **Contracts and agreements**: Discuss and obtain acceptance before any arrangement requires OSE to sign a specific contract.
2. **Grant applications**: If OSE will be named as recipient or signatory, contact the team before submission so it can review and accept its role and commitments.
3. **Community updates**: Use the Updates feature to keep your community informed. Publish at least one update per year.
4. **Expenses**: Follow the [expense guide](/docs/how-it-works/expenses/) for documents, review and payment.
5. **Contributions**: OSE primarily supports donations and grants. Before committing to sales, invoiced services or contributions with promised benefits, contact OSE to discuss whether the arrangement can be supported and under what conditions. Do this before making commitments or receiving payment; acceptance is not guaranteed.

***

## Understanding Open Collective

**Open Collective** is the platform that OSE operates on. It provides tools for transparent budgeting, customizing a Collective profile, setting up tiers and goals, and more.

For an overview of using the Open Collective platform, check out its [documentation](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/about/documentation). It has a wealth of information about the features at your disposal.

Their [Quick Start Guide](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/collectives/quick-start-guide) is another great place to start.

***

## Budget Management of Your Collective

Both receiving funds and submitting expenses are straightforward procedures. You will find the details on both in the respective pages of these docs. Feel free to share them with the contributors and members of your Collective.

Being fiscally hosted by OSE means joining a community of hundreds of initiatives that share the same tools to manage their independent projects. It requires minimal attention, but still some attention, to function properly.

Please familiarize yourself with the guidelines on [donations](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/collectives/raising-money) and [expenses](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/expenses-and-getting-paid/submitting-expenses), and feel free to contact us if you have any questions or doubts at <home@opensourceeurope.org>.

***

## Important Notes

* **Tax Obligations**: You’re responsible for understanding and fulfilling all of your tax obligations. If you’re not sure whether you need to charge or pay tax, we encourage you to consult a tax professional to better understand your tax obligations. OSE can’t provide tax advice.
* **Terms & Conditions**: Please review our [Terms & Conditions](/docs/terms-of-service/) for detailed information about your responsibilities and our policies.

## Keeping your contacts up to date

When administrators or the main contact change, update access on Open Collective and notify OSE so the team can maintain continuity of support.

## Optional welcome call

After acceptance, OSE offers an optional welcome call. Contributions can start without waiting for the call. See [How to apply](/docs/getting-started/how-to-apply/).
