This page lists more ways to raise funds.

Open Collective includes crowdfunding tools and is the usual way to raise funds for a Collective hosted by Open Source Europe (OSE).

## External platforms

OSE considers external platforms, such as Patreon, Ko-fi, and GoFundMe, case by case. Contact OSE before you set up a campaign to receive funds through OSE. The OSE team checks payout arrangements, available records, and any promised benefits. Then the team confirms whether OSE can accept the setup. Do not assume that donations reach your OSE Collective when you choose an organization with a similar name.

## Fundraising support

The Collective leads its fundraising. OSE may occasionally share opportunities or make introductions when it has capacity. OSE does not guarantee funding or provide a standard fundraising service.

For an unidentified incoming payment, [contact OSE](/docs/about/contact-us/) with its reference, so the team can find out where the payment should go. This process is separate from how OSE handles the balances of inactive Collectives.
