Can I refund a donation or get a refund for one I have made?

## Can I request a refund for a donation?

Yes, under certain conditions. Open Source Europe (OSE) can process refunds for contributions made by credit card, bank transfer, or PayPal.

### Refund timeframes

For a credit card or bank transfer contribution, you can request a refund within 90 days of the contribution date. For a PayPal contribution, you can request a refund within 60 days of the contribution date.

:::danger
Refunds go only to the original payment method. For card payments processed through Stripe, Stripe and the card issuer route the refund, including when the original card has expired or been replaced. OSE reviews the request and starts an approved refund. OSE does not control how the bank handles the funds.
:::

***

## How to request a refund

Contact the Collective administrators first. They forward your request to OSE. If the administrators do not respond, contact OSE directly at <home@opensourceeurope.org>. A request does not guarantee a refund. Include the following details:

* URL of the donation (for example, `https://opencollective.com/europe/contributions/528520`)
* Date and amount of the transaction
* Reason for the refund request

:::danger
OSE reviews each refund request. OSE may decline the refund if the contribution was authorized and the funds were already used for the Collective's activities.
:::

***

## Refund processing times

OSE first reviews the refund request. If OSE approves the request, OSE starts the refund. The time it takes for the funds to reach you then depends on the payment method, payment provider, and bank. OSE does not guarantee a fixed processing or receipt date.

These processing steps are separate from the request deadlines in "Refund timeframes": 90 days for card or bank-transfer contributions and 60 days for PayPal.

***

## Additional information

The following points also apply to refunds:

* Refunds go only to the original payment method.
* The handling of an expired card or closed account depends on the payment provider and bank. An expired card or closed account is not an automatic reason for OSE to declare a refund impossible.
* For contributions made by manual bank transfer, the administrators forward the request to OSE to discuss refund options. If the administrators do not respond, contact OSE directly.

***

## Get help

If you have questions about refunds or need more help, contact OSE at <home@opensourceeurope.org>.
