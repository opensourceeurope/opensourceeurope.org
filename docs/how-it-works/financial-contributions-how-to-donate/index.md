Guides for donors and financial contributors.

Choose the route that matches the donor or funding arrangement. For an ordinary contribution, start on the Collective's Open Collective page. The section "Accepted payment methods" describes the card, PayPal, and bank transfer options.

## Why donate through Open Source Europe

Through OSE, donors can support Collectives transparently and securely. Hosted Collectives don't need their own bank account or legal entity: OSE receives and manages the funds for them. Donors can also choose to be publicly acknowledged on the Collective’s profile.

***

## Accepted payment methods

You can make a contribution through:

* Credit or debit card
* PayPal
* Bank transfer (SEPA or international)

:::caution
When you make a bank transfer, include the name of the Collective and your order number in the payment reference. If you cannot, contact OSE at <home@opensourceeurope.org> with your transaction details, so OSE can assign the payment correctly.
:::

***

## Information required

When you donate, the donation form may ask for:

* Your full name
* Your email address
* Your organization name (if applicable)
* Your payment method details

***

## Bank transfer instructions

To contribute by bank transfer, follow the step-by-step [wire transfer instructions](/docs/how-it-works/financial-contributions-how-to-donate/wire-transfer-instructions-via-open-collective-platform/).

***

## Tax-deductible giving

U.S. donors can use the established Myriad → OCEF → OSE route for tax-deductible giving, subject to applicable tax rules. Contact OSE before you pay, so that OSE can organize the right route. For more information, see [U.S. giving](/docs/what-we-offer/tax-deductible-giving-for-us-donors/).

***

## Get help

If you’re not sure which method to use, or you want to check your transaction, contact OSE at <home@opensourceeurope.org>.
