## An established route for U.S. giving

U.S. individuals and businesses can support Collectives hosted by Open Source Europe (OSE) through Myriad USA → Open Collective Europe Foundation (OCEF) → OSE. This established arrangement allows U.S. tax-deductible donations, within the limits of the applicable tax rules. The donor’s actual deduction depends on their circumstances.

## Choose the appropriate donation route

Contact OSE at <home@opensourceeurope.org> before payment, and give the name of the Collective you want to support. The team organizes the route, reference, documents, applicable costs, and transfer arrangements.

* For regular fundraising from U.S. donors, an Every.org page organized with OSE is the preferred option. Funds follow Every.org → Myriad → OCEF → OSE.
* A donor who gives a larger donation gives directly to Myriad, without Every.org. Funds then follow Myriad → OCEF → OSE. Ask the team for the correct instructions. This page gives no fixed amount threshold.

A donation through the ordinary Open Collective checkout does not automatically use this arrangement. Confirm which organization issues the donation documentation for the route you choose.

The [current OSE hosting fees](/docs/faq/general-faqs/#how-much-does-it-cost) apply. The team confirms any additional costs of the route.

## Other cases

If the tax-deductible route for your circumstances is not confirmed yet, contact OSE before you give, and check the available route and documents. OSE does not guarantee an individual tax benefit.

Cryptocurrency donations must start on Every.org. For more information, see [Cryptocurrency contributions](/docs/how-it-works/financial-contributions-how-to-donate/contributions-in-cryptocurrencies/).

For grants from U.S. foundations, see [Equivalency Determination certificate](/docs/what-we-offer/equivalency-determination-certificate/).

For more information about Myriad, see the [Myriad FAQ](https://myriadusa.org/about/faqs/) and the [Myriad ways to give](https://myriadusa.org/waystogive/).
