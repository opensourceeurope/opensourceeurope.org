Open Collective is a shared tool to manage contributions, expenses, and grants with full transparency.

## A transparent financial and community management tool

Open Collective is a digital platform that lets hosted initiatives and philanthropic partners raise, manage, and distribute funds with full transparency. Each group or fund hosted by Open Source Europe (OSE) gets its own public page, with tools to:

* Accept donations by credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer, and manage grants
* Submit, approve, and reimburse expenses directly on the platform
* Display a transparent budget that shows all income and expenditures in real time, which builds trust and accountability
* Post updates, host events, and share progress with supporters

These tools in one place make financial administration simpler and help your initiative or fund work together.

## Support for philanthropic regranting

Grassroots initiatives use the platform, and foundations and funders can use it too. Through Open Collective, OSE can set up dedicated online funds to:

* Receive donations or grants earmarked for specific causes or regions
* Disburse grants to multiple projects (including informal or unincorporated groups) in a simple, transparent way
* Let funders and the public track how resources are used

Each fund has its own profile page, budget, and update feed, like a Collective. Regranting partners can then easily share their goals, track disbursements, and meet reporting obligations with little extra work.

## Why transparency matters

Transparency is central to the Open Collective platform. When financial information is public, initiatives and funders can:

* Show responsible fund management to donors and stakeholders
* Simplify reporting, because funders can directly monitor how their resources are allocated
* Encourage trust and coordination between grantees and grantmakers

Financial transactions are public, but personal details such as names and email addresses are visible only to authorized admins.

## Key features

The Open Collective platform includes:

* Real-time budget tracking
* Integrated expense and payout tools
* Custom donation tiers and fundraising campaigns
* Event management tools
* Automated monthly financial summaries
* Public updates and communication channels
