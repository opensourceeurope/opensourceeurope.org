A shared tool for managing contributions, expenses, and grants with full transparency.

## A transparent financial and community management tool

Open Collective is a digital platform that lets hosted initiatives and philanthropic partners raise, manage, and distribute funds with full transparency. Each group or fund hosted by Open Source Europe gets its own public page, with tools to:

* Collect financial contributions: Accept donations via credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer, and manage grants.
* Manage expenses and disbursements: Submit, approve, and reimburse expenses directly through the platform.
* Display a transparent budget: All income and expenditures are visible in real time, which builds trust and accountability.
* Engage with the community: Post updates, host events, and share progress with supporters.

Having these tools in one place makes financial administration simpler and helps your initiative or fund work together.

## Support for Philanthropic Regranting

Foundations and funders can use the platform too, not only grassroots initiatives. Through Open Collective, OSE can set up dedicated online funds to:

* Receive donations or grants earmarked for specific causes or regions
* Disburse grants to multiple projects (including informal or unincorporated groups) in a simple, transparent way
* Enable funders and the public to track how resources are used

Each fund has its own profile page, budget, and update feed, just like a Collective. Regranting partners can then easily share their goals, track disbursements, and meet reporting obligations with little extra work.

## Why transparency matters

Transparency is central to the Open Collective platform. When financial information is public, initiatives and funders can:

* Build credibility: Show responsible fund management to donors and stakeholders
* Simplify reporting: Funders can directly monitor how their resources are allocated
* Improve collaboration: Encourage trust and coordination between grantees and grantmakers

Financial transactions are public, but personal details like names and email addresses are only visible to authorized admins.

## Key Features

* Real-time budget tracking
* Integrated expense and payout tools
* Custom donation tiers and fundraising campaigns
* Event management tools
* Automated monthly financial summaries
* Public updates and communication channels
