## Grants from U.S. foundations

Open Source Europe (OSE) helps hosted projects receive grants from U.S. foundations through an Equivalency Determination (ED) arrangement.

Contact the OSE team before you apply. The OSE team coordinates with the funder, provides the relevant documentation, and arranges for the grant to reach your project.

## Prepare a grant application

Contact <home@opensourceeurope.org> early, with:

* The funder's name and the project that the grant is for
* Proposed amount, purpose, and application deadline
* Any eligibility requirements or draft grant agreement

The team provides the current ED documentation and confirms the details to use in the grant agreement. An ED does not guarantee that a particular funder or grant program will accept an application.

## Donations from individuals

An ED is different from the route for U.S. tax-deductible donations through Myriad USA. For more information, see [Tax-deductible giving for U.S. donors](/docs/what-we-offer/tax-deductible-giving-for-us-donors/).

## Documentation for the funder

Open Collective Europe Foundation holds the ED used for this arrangement. On this route, the foundation receives the grant and transfers it to OSE for your project. The OSE team coordinates these steps.
