Use this guide to prepare and submit a hosting application to Open Source Europe (OSE), and to learn how OSE reviews it.

## Start here

Follow these steps to apply:

1. Read the [eligibility criteria](/docs/getting-started/how-to-apply/eligibility/), [hosting fees](/docs/faq/general-faqs/#how-much-does-it-cost), and [application questions](/docs/getting-started/how-to-apply/application-questions/).
2. Prepare your project details and at least one administrator. OSE recommends two.
3. Apply on Open Collective, then fill in the OSE application form that OSE emails you. You need both steps. An email to OSE does not start an application.

Next, OSE aims to give a first response within seven business days after it receives the completed application form. The seven days are a target for the response, not a deadline for acceptance. After acceptance, continue with [your first steps](/docs/how-it-works/basics/).

## Join Open Source Europe in a few steps

The application to OSE usually takes a few minutes. After OSE accepts you, you can receive contributions and manage your initiative in the open on the Open Collective platform.

***

## Before you apply

Check that your initiative meets these criteria:

* The initiative aligns with OSE's mission and impact areas. Its activities should be charitable and aim to benefit the public.
* It has at least one administrator. One is enough, and OSE recommends two for shared management and continuity. OSE must know the administrators' real identities. A public pseudonym is allowed. See [Administrators' legal names](/docs/getting-started/how-to-apply/application-questions/#administrators-legal-names).
* For ordinary fiscal hosting, the initiative has no legal entity of its own. An existing legal entity can instead discuss a Fund for a specific program with OSE. This option depends on OSE's review and agreement on the program and on how the Fund operates.
* Its base can be anywhere in the world, not only in Europe. It keeps its balance in euros, and contributions in other currencies arrive in the balance as euros. See [Which currency does a Collective use?](/docs/faq/general-faqs/#which-currency-does-a-collective-use)

Also read the [Terms of Service](/docs/terms-of-service/) and the [community guidelines](/docs/about/community-guidelines/).

***

## Application process

Apply on Open Collective with these steps:

1. Go to the [Open Source Europe profile](https://opencollective.com/europe) and click **Apply**.
2. Choose your Collective type:
   * If you are new to the Open Collective platform, select **Create New Collective**.
   * For an existing Collective, choose it from the list.
3. Complete the application form. Give information about your initiative, including:
   * Location
   * Name and email address
   * Collective name
   * Links to your website or social media
   * Short description of your Collective's activities
   * Explanation of how your Collective furthers OSE's mission impact areas
4. Add at least one administrator. OSE recommends two for shared management and continuity.
5. Review your information, accept the terms of service, and submit your application.

You can edit all this information later, so it does not need to be perfect.

***

## After submission

After you apply on Open Collective, OSE emails you a link to the OSE application form. The form takes about ten minutes. The form asks for the detail that the Open Collective application does not cover, such as the license, the budget, and any company connected to the project. For the full list, see [Questions in the OSE application form](/docs/getting-started/how-to-apply/application-questions/#questions-in-the-ose-application-form).

The review starts when OSE has both the Open Collective application and the form. The form link belongs to your application on Open Collective, so the form does not work for someone who has not applied there. If you have a question about the form, reply to the email that brought you the link.

The following timeline applies when the form does not come back:

1. After seven days, OSE sends one reminder with the link.
2. After seven more days, a person from the OSE team takes over the application. They may close it for lack of a response, and they tell you by email.

A closed application is not a rejection of the project. To resume it, contact OSE at any time.

OSE aims to give a first response within seven business days after it receives your completed form. The review may take longer if OSE needs more information. This target does not guarantee acceptance, or that the review ends within seven days.

After acceptance, you can start to receive contributions. OSE offers an optional welcome call. Contributions can start without waiting for the call.

***

## Move from another fiscal host

If you move from another fiscal host, the process depends on your current platform:

* On the Open Collective platform, the move is fairly simple.
* Outside the Open Collective platform, the process depends on what your current organization requires. Usually, your current fiscal host can transfer your funds to OSE, and you can start quickly.

Tell both OSE and your current fiscal host about your plans before you apply, so that the move goes smoothly.
