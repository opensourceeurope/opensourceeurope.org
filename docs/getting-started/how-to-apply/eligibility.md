## Who can apply

Open Source Europe (OSE) welcomes initiatives from around the world that align with its mission and values. OSE supports open source projects and communities. Your application should explain how your activities contribute to open source. OSE can accept an initiative before it publishes its first code or other open resources. OSE first reviews the initiative and its commitments to openness.

OSE supports both the development of open source and its sustainability. A project does not have to write software to fit. The following initiatives also fit OSE:

* Groups that run an instance of open source software, such as a Matrix homeserver, a mail service, a git forge, or a mirror
* Communities, events, or archives connected to open source

The project's work must use an open license. For software, use a license that the [Open Source Initiative](https://opensource.org/licenses) approves. For data and content, use a license that conforms to the [Open Definition](https://opendefinition.org/licenses/).

An initiative with no connection to open source fits better with OSE's sister host, [Open Collective Europe Foundation](https://opencollective.com/oce-foundation-eur). Civil-society groups and mutual-aid groups are examples. They can apply there instead.

***

## Eligibility criteria

To be eligible for fiscal hosting by OSE, your initiative must:

* Align with OSE's mission and impact areas. Your activities should be charitable and aim to benefit the public.
* Be nonprofit. The initiative must not distribute profits to members. For the types of contributions that OSE accepts, including the rules on membership benefits and other counterparty benefits, see [Contributions FAQ](/docs/faq/contributions-faq/).
* Have no separate legal entity, for ordinary fiscal hosting. Initiatives with their own legal status, such as registered nongovernmental organizations (NGOs), are not eligible for ordinary fiscal hosting. They can set up a Fund with OSE for a specific program. OSE must first review and agree to the program and how the Fund operates. Contact OSE to discuss the arrangement. OSE does not accept the arrangement automatically.
* Have at least one administrator. One is enough, and OSE recommends two for shared management and continuity. OSE must know the administrators' real identities. A public pseudonym is allowed.
* Keep to appropriate content standards. OSE does not permit content that promotes violence, hate speech, pornography, or any other material considered inappropriate or offensive.

***

## Global reach

OSE welcomes applications from around the world. The team assesses which hosting and payment arrangements are available for each application. A project's location alone does not guarantee that hosting or payments are possible.

***

## Not required

OSE aims to lower barriers for initiatives with impact, so OSE does not require:

* Application fees
* A minimum budget balance
* A minimum annual fundraising amount
* A fixed duration of commitment
* Complex reporting requirements as a general condition of hosting

OSE recommends an annual update on your Collective page. Reporting requirements in a specific funding arrangement or agreement still apply.

***

## Important considerations

Keep the following points in mind:

* Under the hosting agreement, OSE administers funds on behalf of your initiative. If you move to another host or close, contact OSE. You then agree with OSE how to handle outstanding expenses and the remaining balance, under the applicable agreement and funding restrictions.
* OSE assesses possible compliance risks of initiatives. OSE checks that initiatives align with legal frameworks and avoid activities that could be linked to terrorism or extremism financing.

***

## Ready to apply

If your initiative meets the criteria on this page, follow the [How to apply](/docs/getting-started/how-to-apply/) guide to start your application.

If you have questions, [contact OSE](mailto:home@opensourceeurope.org).

## Affiliation with another organization

OSE does not accept fiscal hosting at the same time as another host. OSE may accept a technical or community affiliation with another organization after review. Affiliation alone does not mean fiscal hosting.
