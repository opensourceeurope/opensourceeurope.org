Questions about contributions to the Collectives that Open Source Europe (OSE) hosts.

## How do I cancel a recurring donation, subscription, or payment?

To cancel a recurring donation, subscription, or other ongoing payment, follow the [Open Collective instructions to change or cancel a contribution](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/financial-contributors/payments#change-the-amount-payment-method-or-cancel-an-existing-contribution).

## What if a contributor wants their contribution to be anonymous?

Contributors can make an anonymous payment by choosing [incognito](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/financial-contributors/payments#profile) when they pick a profile to pay from. If the contributor pays as a [guest](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/financial-contributors/payments#contributing-as-a-guest), the name they give still shows. A guest payment only means that the contributor does not have to log in. All Open Collective users can also [separate a public display name from their private legal name](https://opencollective.com/opencollective/updates/new-legal-and-display-name-settings).

## What if a financial contributor cannot or does not want to submit their credit card information online?

If a donor is not comfortable with online credit card transactions, they can send the funds through PayPal or a bank transfer.

## What if a donation does not appear on the Collective's page?

This answer applies when someone says they sent money to your Collective, and also when you sent money yourself. In both cases, [contact OSE](/docs/about/contact-us/).

OSE often receives money in its bank account without any sign of where the money should go. This is why the order number in the bank transfer is so important. OSE always searches carefully for the right Collective, but sometimes OSE cannot find it.

OSE keeps unidentified funds pending until it can identify the intended Collective. Once OSE identifies the Collective, OSE can allocate the funds to it. There is no fixed monthly transfer schedule for unidentified funds.

To follow up on a bank transfer, email <home@opensourceeurope.org> with these details:

* Transfer receipt
* Amount
* Date
* Sender's name
* Intended Collective
* Order number, if you have it

Discord is a community space. Do not use it for bank transfer cases or supporting documents.

## What if a financial contributor asks for an invoice before they donate?

OSE can confirm receipt of a donation, but a donation confirmation is not a sales invoice or a value-added tax (VAT) invoice. OSE primarily supports donations and grants. Before you commit to sales, invoiced services, or contributions with promised benefits, contact OSE. Discuss with OSE whether it can support the arrangement and under what conditions. Do this before you make commitments or receive payment. Acceptance is not guaranteed.

## Can my Collective use outside fundraising platforms?

Open Collective already includes crowdfunding tools and is the usual option. OSE considers external platforms, such as Patreon, Ko-fi, or GoFundMe, case by case. Contact OSE before you set up a campaign to receive funds through OSE. OSE checks payout arrangements, supporting records, and any promised benefits. Compatibility and acceptance are not automatic.

For the GitHub Sponsors route, see [GitHub Sponsors](/docs/how-it-works/financial-contributions-how-to-donate/setting-up-github-sponsors/).

## Can my Collective receive earned income that is not a donation?

OSE primarily supports donations and grants. Before you commit to sales, invoiced services, or contributions with promised benefits, contact OSE. Discuss with OSE whether it can support the arrangement and under what conditions. Do this before you make commitments or receive payment. Acceptance is not guaranteed.

## Can my Collective offer financial contributors something in return for their donation?

Tiers can suggest contribution amounts. If you intend to promise goods, services, advertising, access, or another benefit, contact OSE before you commit or receive funds. OSE checks whether it can support the arrangement. Acceptance is not guaranteed. You can still acknowledge donors, as long as the acknowledgment is optional and not promised as part of a tier.

## Can my Collective offer memberships or accept membership fees?

A symbolic supporter membership gives no rights or benefits. Discuss any proposed membership with benefits with OSE before you commit to it or collect fees. Acceptance is not automatic.

## Can my Collective donate to another hosted Collective?

Transfers to another hosted Collective are different from ordinary external donations. Before you go ahead, get OSE’s approval of the recipient, purpose, and funding conditions. Collectives hosted by OSE [can give to other Collectives](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/financial-contributors/collective-to-collective) hosted by OSE. To give to a Collective with another fiscal host, follow [the extra steps for Collectives with different fiscal hosts](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/financial-contributors/collective-to-collective#across-different-fiscal-hosts).

## U.S. tax-deductible donations

The established Myriad → OCEF → OSE route supports U.S. tax-deductible giving, subject to applicable tax rules. Contact OSE before payment to organize the right route. For more information, see [U.S. giving](/docs/what-we-offer/tax-deductible-giving-for-us-donors/).

## Donation refunds

Contact the Collective administrators first. They forward the request to OSE. If they do not respond, contact OSE directly. Contact does not guarantee a refund. For more information, see [Refunds](/docs/how-it-works/financial-contributions-how-to-donate/refunds/).
